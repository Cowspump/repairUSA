Questions, answered.

How much is a diagnostic visit?

Diagnostic visits start at $89 in the current Chicago service area. Your master confirms the visit details and any repair estimate before work begins.

How soon can someone call?

Most requests receive a call within 45 minutes during service hours. For urgent issues, choose ASAP in the booking form.

What appliances do you repair?

Washers, dryers, refrigerators, dishwashers, ovens, ranges, cooktops, microwaves and other common household appliances.

Do you cover my ZIP code?

We currently serve Chicago and nearby neighborhoods. Add your five-digit ZIP in the form and we’ll confirm coverage before scheduling.

Is the repair guaranteed?

Warranty details depend on the repair and part used. Your master explains what is covered on the estimate.

Can I book by phone?

Yes. Call (312) 555-0123 and a member of the team will help with the same information.
